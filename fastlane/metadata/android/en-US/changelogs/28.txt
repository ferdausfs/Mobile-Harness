New: watch Z.ai think and answer LIVE in your project chat, and pick the model from the site's own dropdown. While a Z.ai chat-engine turn generates, the chat card now streams what the page is doing about once a second: a collapsible "Thinking" section with the model's reasoning text, the partial answer as it arrives, the latest tool/step line (e.g. searching the web), the current phase and elapsed time - instead of the old "Z.ai is generating" line. The current model (read from chat.z.ai's own label) is shown in the chat header and in the engine picker. Tap Model in the engine picker to open the new model picker: it reads the options from chat.z.ai's own dropdown (cached between sessions), and picking one drives the hidden page's dropdown and is verified by reading the label back - if the automation cannot do it, you get a clear message and the read-only label instead, never a silent failure. Your choice is remembered. Everything else (sign-in, NEED_FILES, review-and-apply, budget) is unchanged; the three CLI agents are untouched.
