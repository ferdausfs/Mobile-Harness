Fixes "the app never updates": the in-app update check ran once per day, silently, and a single failed check (weak network at app start) was counted as a completed check - which blocked every retry for the next 24 hours, while release builds had no button to check again manually. v1.0.21 was in fact published and served correctly the whole time; the app just refused to look. Now: a failed check no longer blocks retries (the next app start checks again automatically), and Settings has a new always-visible App update card with a Check now button, the current version, a clear "you're on the latest version" or failure message, and - when an update exists - the same download flow as before with progress, the Install-unknown-apps permission step and retry. If the app still does not offer the update, Settings > App update > Check now will surface the reason instead of staying silent. Also carries all of v1.0.21 (the Z.ai sign-in session fix + Diagnostics). Same signing key - installs over v1.0.20/v1.0.21 with one tap.
