Fixes the update flow you hit this week: (1) In-app updates now RESUME instead of restarting - the download continues from where your connection dropped (up to 5 attempts with backoff), only hands Android a fully downloaded and SHA-256-verified APK, and each update gets its own partial file so two versions can never mix. This is the permanent fix for "download complete hoy na" on networks that cannot carry an ~88 MB file in one go - do updates from Settings > App update, not the browser. (2) Crashes are no longer a blind spot: the app now saves the stack trace of any crash to storage, and Settings shows a "Last crash" card with Copy report - send that report after a crash and the cause is known immediately instead of guessed. (3) Hardened the installer hand-off and the "Install unknown apps" round-trip so neither can crash the app. Still the same signing key - installs over v1.0.22/v1.0.23 with one tap.
