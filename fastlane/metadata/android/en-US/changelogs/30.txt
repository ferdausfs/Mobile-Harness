New: Z.ai tasks keep running with the screen off. A proper dataSync foreground service now holds the engine open while a Z.ai chat task generates: a persistent "Z.ai is working" notification shows the live phase/tool step/elapsed and carries a working Stop button, and the result comes back as a notification ("Z.ai answered", "Z.ai needs your attention" for sign-in or captcha, or the failure reason). Android's 6-hour background limit for data sync is handled gracefully - before it hits, the task queue is checkpointed and you are notified instead of the app dying silently. The task queue itself is now persisted: every chat-engine task is saved before it is sent, so if the app is ever killed, the next start reports exactly what did not finish (notification) and a task queued within the last 10 minutes is re-sent automatically once you are signed in again - it never re-runs anything that already started. The CLI agents keep their own foreground service; nothing about them changed.
