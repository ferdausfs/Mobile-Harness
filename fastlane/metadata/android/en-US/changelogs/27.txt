Fix: the in-app updater no longer crashes the moment a download starts. On 1.0.24/1.0.25 every in-app update from Settings > App update died with UnknownFormatConversionException as soon as the download size became known - the progress text fed a pattern ending in a lone '%' to String.format. Progress lines are now built without ever using visible text as a format pattern, and a new source-scan test guards against that whole bug class. Download resume, the SHA-256 check and the installer hand-off are unchanged, and the Last crash card in Settings still lets you copy and clear a crash report. Same signing key - installs over 1.0.25 with one tap.
