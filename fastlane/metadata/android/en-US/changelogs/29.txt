New: Settings > Tokens - your own token vault. Add named tokens for GitHub (GITHUB_TOKEN), Cloudflare (CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN + optional account id), Vercel, Firebase, Supabase, npm, or any custom env var name. Values are encrypted with the Android keystore, shown only masked, and never sent to any model: everything leaving the app (prompts to chat.z.ai, transcripts, crash reports, logs, clipboard exports) has token values - and common token shapes like ghp_/github_pat_/glpat- - replaced by ***. A "Test token" button runs a read-only provider call with a plain-language result. After every task that had tokens enabled you get a clear "Rotate these tokens now: GitHub, Cloudflare..." card and notification with a button per provider that opens its token management page; the token keeps a "needs rotation" badge in Settings until you tap Rotated or replace it, and an optional per-token auto-disable switches it off after the task. Nothing is ever deleted without your tap. Notifications now ask for permission once at first start. The Z.ai live streaming card and model picker from v1.0.27 are unchanged.
